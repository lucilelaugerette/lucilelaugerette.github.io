About

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at ENS de Lyon, in the Center for Economic Research on Governance, Inequality and Conflict (CERGIC), supervised by Mathieu Couttenier. I will be on the 2026–2027 academic job market.

My research lies at the intersection of development economics, political economy and media economics. I study how narratives, whether news coverage or leaders' speeches, shape behavior, with a particular focus on conflict and gender-based violence.
