Dear Brothers and Sisters: Pope's Speeches and the Dynamics of Conflict in Africa

With Mathieu Couttenier, Sophie Hatte, and Tommaso Sonno

Abstract
We study the effect of papal peace-promoting speeches on conflict dynamics in Africa over the period 1997–2022. We find that papal speeches reduce overall conflict by 23% on average. Effects vary by Pope and by the regional presence of Catholics.

Groundwater, Climate Change and Conflict: Empirical Evidence from Africa

With Mathieu Couttenier and Remi Jedwab

Forthcoming, World Bank Economic Review

Abstract
We study the relationship between groundwater resources and conflict in Africa over the period 1997–2021. Analyzing 10,310 cells across the continent, we find that areas with a higher proportion of shallow groundwater have become increasingly prone to violence over time, particularly in the presence of persistent local climate shocks.
